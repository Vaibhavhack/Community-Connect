## Viva demonstration sequence

1. Register two users.
2. Login as the resource owner.
3. Add an educational resource.
4. Login as another user.
5. Create a request for the resource.
6. Show the owner notification.
7. Show that contact information is blocked before acceptance.
8. Accept the request.
9. Show that contact information is now available to the authorized party.
10. Complete the exchange.
11. Show the final notification.
12. Create High, Medium and Low requests and demonstrate heap ordering.
13. Stop and restart the server.
14. Show that the `.txt` records persist.
